Baget support

Email bagetsupport@gmail.com. We usually reply within a day or two.

In the app, you can also go to Profile → Send feedback, or shake your phone to report a problem with a screenshot.

Common questions

My scouts haven't found anything yet. A new scout runs its first hunt within a few minutes. On Free, scouts hunt once a day during your 4-hour hunting window, which you can change in Settings. On Pro, they hunt every 6 hours, around the clock.

How do I cancel Baget Pro? Go to Profile → Manage subscription, or open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.

I paid but don't have Pro. Go to Profile → Go Pro → Restore purchases. If that doesn't work, email us with roughly when you bought it.

How do I delete my account? Go to Profile → Delete my account. This removes your account, scouts, finds, photos, chats and feedback from our servers right away.

Does Baget buy things for me? No. Baget finds products and links you to the store. You always check out yourself.
